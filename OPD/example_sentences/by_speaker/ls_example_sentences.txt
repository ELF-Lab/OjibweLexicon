Mii ko ingiw ikwewag naadamaagewaad nagamong mii imaa zhaabowewaad ezhi-wiinjigaadeg. Mii ko o'ow waabooyaan naa asemaan asaad da-bagijiged awiya. Miskwaamagad mbiizikawaagan. Mii imaa ogijayi'ii odaabaaning goon abid. Booch noongom a'aw niibowa anishinaabe da-ni-aanikanootamawind i'iw ojibwemowin. Gegaa go ngii-wanendaan apii gaa-tibishkaayaan. Niwii-poonitoon minikweyaan ow zhingobaaboo. Ingiw bebezhigooganzhiig inganawaabamaag. Ningii-wiidookaagoog indinawemaaganag gii-ayaanziwaan ge-miijiyaan. Nimikwendaan ko gaa-inaajimotaagooyaan. Mii imaa dibendaagoziyaan dewe'iganing niniigaani-niimi'iwewininiiw.
