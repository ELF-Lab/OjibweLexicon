Madweyaakwa'ige ako baapaase endaayaan. Bibigwe migizi. Desaashi wiinaange. Ogidaagonag bimose bizhiw. Zaaziibiskaaj bimi-bazigwii. Giizis endaso-giizhik biidaasige. Nitaa-googii maang. Nashke awedi amik biidaatiga'o. Inendam jigijigaaneshiinh wiikojitood ge-miijid. Minonaagozi ozhaawashkozid diindiisi. Ginwaabiigigwe waabizii. Wegonen neboobiikaadang. Engwaamizin, giga-gimoodimig gimiijim gwiingwiishi. Hay', ingii-peshaabikinaan. Anang gii-piidagoojin. Mitakamigaang baa-moozhaganjige mooningwane. Obiinitoon aki gayaashk. Oshpiming inaabi mooshka'osi goshko'ind. Onitaa-ozhi'aan opwaaganan. Ingoding ngo-giizhik eta go dazhitaa ji-giizhi'aad. Bimaabiigamo'idiwag nikag. Ombiigweyaakwa'ige meme mitigong. Mii 'i niizhogon giikanaabasog name. Niwii-chiibaakwaanaa zhigo. Ingii-peshaabikinaan mookomaanish.
